Teeth that feel like your own again

Dr Wadhwani’s Dental Implant Centre combines over 20 years of extensive surgical experience with a commitment to restoring smiles through dental implant treatment. Specialising in the rehabilitation of patients with one or more missing teeth, the centre focuses on personalised treatment planning, surgical precision, and long-term oral health. With an emphasis on ethical practice, patient comfort, and functional and aesthetic outcomes, the centre strives to help patients regain their ability to chew comfortably, smile confidently, and enjoy a better quality of life.

Our Expertise

Dr Wadhwani’s Dental Implant Centre offers comprehensive expertise across the entire spectrum of dental implant treatment. Our services range from single-tooth implants and multiple-teeth replacement to full-mouth rehabilitation for patients requiring extensive restoration of their teeth. We also specialise in hard and soft tissue grafting procedures, which may be required to address deficiencies in bone and gum tissue and facilitate implant placement. Recognising that successful implant treatment extends beyond surgery, we emphasise regular implant follow-ups to monitor healing, assess implant health, and maintain long-term oral function.

Single tooth implant

Replace one missing tooth without affecting the teeth beside it.

Multiple teeth implants

Fixed teeth for several missing teeth in a row.

Full mouth rehabilitation

A complete set of fixed teeth supported by implants.

Hard and soft tissue management

Grafting of bone and gum tissue where needed, so an implant can be placed well.

Meet Dr. Wadhwani

Oral and maxillofacial surgeon and dental implant specialist.

Dr. Puneet Wadhwani

Founder and implant surgeon · MDS, Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

Years of Experience

  • Total surgical and professional experience: Over 20 years of extensive surgical experience.
  • Academic employment: Approximately 20 years of academic experience holding positions including Senior Resident, Senior Lecturer, Reader, and Professor in the Department of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery across institutions like K.G.U.D.S. (Lucknow), Saraswati Dental College, Chandra Dental College, and Career Post Graduate Institute and Dental Sciences and Hospitals.
  • Specialty training and residencies: Includes hands-on residency and intensive training at Meenakshi Ammal Dental College (Chennai), Swiss Cleft & Craniofacial Centre (Chennai), Charles Pinto Centre, and Nizams Institute of Medical Sciences (Hyderabad).

Qualifications & Academic Background

Degrees & certifications

  • PhD: Pursuing from MAHER University, Chennai.
  • MDS: Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery from Meenakshi Ammal Dental College, Tamil Nadu (2004).
  • BDS: DAV (C) Dental College, Yamunanagar, Haryana (2000).
  • Senior Residency: K.G.U.D.S., Lucknow (2005).
  • Fellowship: TMJ Foundation DAMER India (2024).
  • Life Membership: Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (AOMSI).
  • Certifications: BLS (Basic Life Support) and ACLS (Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support) from the American Heart Association.

Key professional recognition & achievements

  • Over 30 national and international research article publications.
  • Recipient of multiple prestigious awards, including the Gowmathi Radhakrishnana Distinguished Alumni Award (2022) and the Best Outgoing Post Graduate in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery medal (2005).

Our guiding inspiration

Prof. (Mrs.) K. K. Wadhwani

Senior Professor and former Head, Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics · MDS, Operative Dentistry

Dr. Puneet Wadhwani’s mother, and a senior doctor whose career in dental education and endodontics spans more than three decades at King George’s Medical University, Lucknow.
